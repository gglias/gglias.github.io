How I work

  1. Step 1

    Understand

    Talk to the people who'll use it and map what they need to get done.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    Wireframes first, then UI in Figma, built from components that map one-to-one to code.

  3. Step 3

    Prototype

    Clickable flows we can test and change before anyone writes a line of code.

  4. Step 4

    Build

    WordPress, Elementor and custom plugins where they're needed. Multilingual from day one.

About

Most handoffs lose something. A spacing value, a hover state, the way a menu should feel on a phone. I avoid that by doing both halves: I design the interface, and then I'm the one who builds it.

At Smartup that means client websites, our own site, and a small family of WordPress plugins that do one job each and do it well.
